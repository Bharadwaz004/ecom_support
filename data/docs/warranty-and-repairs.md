# Warranty and Repairs

## Warranty periods

Warranty is provided by the manufacturer, not by DesiCart. Standard periods by category:

- Electronics: 12 months manufacturer warranty.
- Large appliances: 24 months manufacturer warranty.
- Small appliances: 6 months manufacturer warranty.
- Furniture: 12 months manufacturer warranty.
- Apparel: no warranty; covered only by the return policy.
- Home: no warranty; covered only by the return policy.
- Beauty: no warranty; covered only by the return policy.
- Grocery: no warranty; covered only by the return policy.
- Toys: no warranty; covered only by the return policy.

The warranty starts on the delivery date recorded against your order. Your DesiCart invoice
is valid proof of purchase at any authorised service centre.

## What warranty covers

Manufacturing defects, component failure under normal use, and functional faults present
from the start. If the fault appears within 7 days of delivery you can
choose a replacement or refund instead of a repair.

## What warranty does not cover

Physical damage, liquid damage, damage from a power surge, normal wear, consumables such as
batteries and filters, cosmetic marks, and any product opened or repaired by an
unauthorised technician. Loss or theft is never covered.

## Raising a warranty claim

Raise the claim from the order page or contact the manufacturer's service centre directly
with the DesiCart invoice. We respond to claims raised through DesiCart within
3 business days with a service centre reference or a pickup
slot. Turnaround at the service centre depends on the manufacturer.

## Replacement under warranty

If the service centre declares the item beyond economical repair, the manufacturer issues a
replacement of the same or an equivalent model. If no equivalent exists, DesiCart refunds the
invoice value to your original payment method within 3 to 5 business days of
receiving the service centre's report.
